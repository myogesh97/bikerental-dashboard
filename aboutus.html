Our Story: From Guwahati's
Roads to a Rider's Paradise

Our journey didn't start in a boardroom; it started on the winding roads of Assam. As passionate local riders, we wanted to share the unparalleled beauty of our home with the world. We're more than a rental shop in Guwahati—we are your local guides, your support crew, and your fellow adventurers, dedicated to making your Northeast India journey unforgettable.

We believe a motorcycle is the ultimate key to freedom, unlocking experiences and creating memories that last a lifetime. That's why we focus on providing perfectly maintained bikes and authentic, local knowledge.
